BU International Center Workspace V4.6.8

Faculty/Major Excel helper-column cleanup

- Renamed the two legacy fallback/helper columns:
  - Faculty EN Copy -> (auto) Faculty EN Copy
  - Major EN Copy -> (auto) Major EN Copy
- The two (auto) columns are collapsed to zero width in the bundled Reference_Data.xlsx so normal users do not see or edit them during routine maintenance.
- Faculty EN and Major EN remain the authoritative editable fields.
- Website import logic supports both the new (auto) headers and the old legacy headers, so previously saved/imported workbooks remain compatible.
- Bundled Reference_Data.xlsx, data/Reference_Data.xlsx, reference-snapshot.json, and reference-snapshot.js are synchronized.
