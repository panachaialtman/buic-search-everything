BU International Center Workspace V4.6.7

Faculty/Major Excel import authority fix

Problem fixed
- Faculty/Major cards previously preferred the legacy helper columns "Faculty EN Copy" and "Major EN Copy" over the editable primary Excel columns "Faculty EN" and "Major EN".
- As a result, a user could edit Major EN in Excel, successfully import the workbook, and still see the old title on the website.

New rule
- Faculty EN and Major EN are now the authoritative editable fields.
- Faculty EN Copy and Major EN Copy are fallback-only for legacy rows where the primary field is blank.
- The rule is applied consistently to search result titles, expanded cards, floating cards, search suggestions, Copy all output, width/layout calculations, and Communication program datalists.

Bundled data update
The following four Major EN display values are included in the bundled workbook and snapshot:
- Entrepreneurship (International Program)
- Innovative Media Production (International Program)
- Culinary Arts and Design (International Program)
- Film, Series and Global Content Production and Business (International Program)

Compatibility
- The V4.6.6 browser-storage keys are intentionally retained so a workbook already imported by the user can continue to load. Once V4.6.7 code reads it, edits in the primary Faculty EN / Major EN columns take precedence automatically.
